# Huerray Help Center: Creators and Brands

This guide is written for Creator and Brand users on Huerray. It explains the main dashboard workflows, account settings, content submission steps, payments, payouts, and common troubleshooting paths.

For urgent issues or anything that cannot be handled from the dashboard, contact Huerray support at support@huerray.de or submit a case from the Help area in your dashboard.

## Creator Help

Creators use Huerray to find brand collaborations, apply to gigs, submit content, manage earnings, and keep profile and payout details up to date.

### Getting Started as a Creator

1. Create a Creator account from the signup page.
2. Verify your email address if prompted.
3. Complete your Creator profile with accurate personal, content, social, and portfolio information.
4. Add your preferred categories, content styles, languages, and any relevant audience details.
5. Add bank details before expecting payouts.
6. Browse available gigs or review invitations from brands.
7. Apply only to gigs where you can meet the brief, timeline, and content requirements.

Your profile quality affects how brands evaluate you. Keep your bio, portfolio examples, social links, content categories, and profile image current.

### Creator Dashboard Overview

The Creator dashboard may include:

- Dashboard: A summary of your current activity.
- Invitations: Gigs that brands have invited you to join.
- Gigs: Available, active, and past gig opportunities.
- My Gigs: Gigs you have applied to, accepted, or are currently working on.
- Analytics: Performance and activity insights where available.
- Earnings: Approved earnings and payout status.
- Cases: Support cases connected to your account, gigs, submissions, or payments.
- Profile: Your public Creator profile details.
- Settings: Account, bank, and security settings.

### Finding and Applying to Gigs

Use the Gigs area to review available collaborations. Before applying, check:

- Brand name and campaign context.
- Required content type, format, and duration.
- Number of videos or deliverables.
- Compensation.
- Product or production requirements.
- Posting requirements, if any.
- Deadline and revision expectations.
- Usage rights and content restrictions.

To apply:

1. Open the gig details.
2. Read the brief fully.
3. Confirm that you can meet the requirements.
4. Submit your application.
5. Watch for brand updates, invitation responses, or approval notifications.

Do not apply if you cannot meet the delivery date, content format, or brand requirements. This helps avoid cancellations, rejected submissions, and delayed earnings.

### Accepting Invitations

Brands can invite Creators directly to a gig. When you receive an invitation:

1. Open Invitations from the dashboard.
2. Review the gig brief, compensation, deliverables, and schedule.
3. Accept the invitation if you want to participate.
4. Decline if the gig is not a fit.
5. Ask for clarification through a case or support channel if the brief is unclear.

After accepting, treat the gig as active work. Confirm product shipping, required assets, deadlines, and any missing creative direction as early as possible.

### Submitting Content

Submit content from the active gig workflow. Uploading content anywhere else can delay review and payment.

Before uploading:

- Review the campaign brief again.
- Confirm the required file format and video duration.
- Check that the video matches the requested orientation, framing, and content style.
- Make sure audio is clear and lighting is usable.
- Remove unrelated logos, copyrighted music, or third-party content unless explicitly approved.
- Confirm all visible people have agreed to appear in the content.
- Check spelling in any captions, on-screen text, or product claims.

Submission steps:

1. Go to Creator dashboard > Gigs.
2. Open the active gig.
3. Find the submission area.
4. Upload the required video or asset files.
5. Add notes if the brand needs context.
6. Submit for review.
7. Monitor notifications and case messages for feedback.

### Content Review and Revisions

After submission, the brand reviews your content against the brief. The submission may be:

- Approved: The content meets the brief and can move toward final delivery and payout.
- Returned for revision: The brand requests changes within the agreed scope.
- Rejected: The content does not meet requirements or cannot be accepted.

Revision guidance:

- Read feedback carefully before recording again.
- Keep changes within the original brief unless Huerray confirms an upgrade.
- Ask for clarification if feedback is vague or conflicts with the brief.
- Submit the revised file through the same gig submission flow.

One revision round is commonly included when feedback is within the brief scope. If the brand requests extra deliverables, a new concept, or additional video work, contact Huerray so the scope and compensation can be reviewed.

### Earnings and Payouts

Creator earnings are tied to approved work. Your fee is shown before you apply to or accept a gig.

Typical payout flow:

1. You complete the content.
2. The brand reviews the submission.
3. The content is approved.
4. Huerray processes the payout.
5. Payment is sent once payout requirements are met.

Important payout notes:

- Payouts are processed after content approval.
- Huerray may issue the required invoice or payout documentation for the approved work.
- Payout timing can depend on approval status, minimum payout thresholds, bank detail accuracy, and internal processing.
- Existing FAQ content states that once the Creator balance reaches EUR 50, payment is transferred within 14 days.

If a payout is delayed, check:

- Whether the content is approved.
- Whether your bank details are complete and accurate.
- Whether your account requires additional verification.
- Whether a support case or payment issue is open.

### Bank Details

Creators should keep bank details updated before submitting paid work.

To update bank details:

1. Go to Creator dashboard > Settings.
2. Open Bank Settings or Bank Details.
3. Enter the required account holder and banking information.
4. Review all details carefully before saving.
5. Save changes and confirm there are no validation errors.

Use legal and accurate details. Incorrect bank information can cause failed or delayed payouts. If your bank account changes while a payout is pending, contact support so the team can confirm the correct next step.

### Updating Your Creator Profile

To update your profile:

1. Go to Creator dashboard > Profile.
2. Edit your personal details, bio, categories, location, languages, and profile image.
3. Add or update social media links.
4. Add portfolio examples where available.
5. Save changes.

Profile best practices:

- Use a clear profile image.
- Keep your name and contact details accurate.
- Add recent examples that match the type of work you want.
- Keep social links active and public enough for brand review.
- Update your profile whenever your content style, equipment, language, or availability changes.

### Creator Account Settings

Creator account settings may include:

- Account details: Name, email, username, and contact information.
- Profile settings: Creator-facing profile fields and portfolio information.
- Bank settings: Payout details.
- Security: Password changes and authentication settings.
- Notifications: Email or in-app notification preferences where available.

Change your password from the security or change-password section. Use password reset from the login page if you cannot access your account.

### Creator Troubleshooting

Use this checklist before contacting support:

- Cannot log in: Use the reset password option and check your email inbox and spam folder.
- Cannot apply to gigs: Confirm your profile is complete and your account is approved.
- Missing invitation: Check Notifications and Invitations, then contact support with the brand or gig name.
- Upload failed: Check file size, file format, internet connection, and try again.
- Feedback unclear: Reply through the case or support thread and ask for specific changes.
- Payout delayed: Confirm content approval, bank details, payout threshold, and payout status.

## Brand Help

Brands use Huerray to create campaigns, invite and manage Creators, review applications, approve submissions, download content, manage invoices, and track payments.

### Getting Started as a Brand

1. Create a Brand account from the signup page.
2. Verify your email address if prompted.
3. Complete your Brand profile with company and contact details.
4. Create your first campaign brief.
5. Add product, target audience, creative direction, content format, budget, and timeline.
6. Submit the campaign for review if required.
7. Review Creator applications or invite Creators directly.
8. Track production, submissions, approvals, and downloads from the campaign dashboard.

Strong briefs lead to stronger content. Include examples, product claims, do-and-don't guidance, pronunciation notes, required shots, and approval constraints upfront.

### Brand Dashboard Overview

The Brand dashboard may include:

- Dashboard: Summary of campaign activity.
- Campaigns: Campaign creation, review, and management.
- Gigs: Creator assignments and deliverables connected to campaigns.
- Creators: Creator discovery, review, and invitations.
- Analytics: Campaign insights where available.
- Invoices: Billing documents and invoice status.
- Payments: Payment history and payment status.
- Cases: Support and issue threads.
- Profile: Brand profile details.
- Settings: Billing, account, and security settings.

### Creating a Campaign

Campaigns should include enough detail for Creators to produce aligned content without repeated clarification.

Recommended campaign details:

- Campaign title and product or service name.
- Campaign goal, such as awareness, conversion, product education, or ad creative testing.
- Target audience.
- Required content type, such as testimonial, unboxing, lifestyle, review, product demo, or ad-style video.
- Number of Creators and number of videos.
- Video format, orientation, duration, and file type requirements.
- Creative tone and messaging.
- Required talking points and prohibited claims.
- Product shipping or access instructions.
- Deadline and launch date.
- Revision expectations.
- Usage rights and paid media needs.
- Budget or package information.

After submission, Huerray may review the campaign to make sure the brief is clear and production-ready.

### Managing Creators

Brands can manage Creators through campaign applications, invitations, and Creator profile views.

Use Creator management to:

- Review incoming applications.
- Compare Creator profile details, content examples, categories, and fit.
- Invite Creators to a specific campaign or gig.
- Accept or reject applications.
- Track invitation status.
- Review active Creator submissions.
- Open support cases connected to a Creator, gig, or campaign.

When choosing Creators, consider:

- Content style and visual quality.
- Relevance to your product category.
- Language, location, or cultural fit.
- Previous examples and profile completeness.
- Ability to follow brief requirements.
- Delivery reliability.

### Reviewing Applications

To review applications:

1. Go to Brand dashboard > Campaigns.
2. Open the campaign.
3. Review applications or Creator recommendations.
4. Open Creator profiles to assess fit.
5. Accept selected Creators.
6. Decline Creators who are not a fit.
7. Confirm next steps for accepted Creators.

Be decisive and clear. Delayed application review can delay production timelines.

### Inviting Creators

To invite Creators:

1. Go to Brand dashboard > Creators or open a campaign.
2. Find Creators that match the brief.
3. Use the invite action for the relevant gig or campaign.
4. Confirm deliverables, compensation, and timeline.
5. Track invitation status.

If you need more Creators than originally booked, contact Huerray so the order, scope, and invoice can be updated.

### Reviewing Submitted Content

After a Creator submits content:

1. Open the campaign or gig.
2. Go to the submissions area.
3. Watch or download the preview if available.
4. Compare the submission against the brief.
5. Approve the content if it meets requirements.
6. Request a revision if changes are needed within scope.
7. Use support cases for disputes, unclear revisions, or scope changes.

Revision requests should be specific. Include timestamps, exact wording changes, missing shots, product corrections, or compliance concerns.

### Downloading Final Content

Final content is generally available after approval and any required payment step is complete.

To download:

1. Open the campaign or gig.
2. Go to the approved submission or assets area.
3. Download the final file.
4. Store the file in your internal asset library.
5. Respect the usage rights and terms attached to the campaign.

Supported formats may include .mov, .avi, .mp4, and .mpeg4, depending on campaign requirements and uploaded files.

### Billing and Payments

Brands can view invoices and payment history from the Brand dashboard.

Billing areas may include:

- Invoices: Invoice documents, status, and download links.
- Payments: Payment records and payment status.
- Billing settings: Billing details, company information, and payment preferences where available.
- Cases: Billing support, invoice questions, and disputes.

Common billing workflow:

1. Create or confirm the campaign scope.
2. Review the campaign cost or package.
3. Complete payment through the available payment method.
4. Receive an invoice.
5. Track invoice and payment status from the dashboard.

Payment methods may include credit card or bank transfer depending on the current checkout and billing setup.

### Updating Brand Profile

To update your Brand profile:

1. Go to Brand dashboard > Profile.
2. Update company name, logo, website, description, category, and contact details.
3. Save changes.
4. Confirm the profile displays correctly.

Your Brand profile helps Creators understand who they are working with. Keep it accurate and current, especially when launching campaigns that require product knowledge or industry context.

### Brand Account Settings

Brand account settings may include:

- Account details: User name, email, and contact information.
- Profile settings: Company-facing brand profile fields.
- Billing settings: Billing information and payment preferences.
- Security: Password changes and account access.
- Notifications: Email or dashboard notification preferences where available.

To add team members, contact Huerray support with the names and email addresses of the colleagues who need access.

### Brand Troubleshooting

Use this checklist before contacting support:

- Campaign not receiving applications: Check if the brief is approved, complete, and attractive to Creators.
- Creator has not accepted invitation: Send a follow-up or invite additional Creators.
- Submission does not match the brief: Request a specific revision within the allowed revision window.
- Need extra videos or Creators: Contact Huerray to update the campaign scope and invoice.
- Invoice missing: Check Brand dashboard > Invoices, then contact support with the campaign name.
- Payment issue: Check payment status and billing details, then submit a case.
- Cannot download approved content: Confirm content approval and payment status.

## Shared Account Help

### Password and Login

If you forgot your password:

1. Go to the login page.
2. Select Reset password.
3. Enter your account email.
4. Follow the email instructions.
5. Set a new secure password.

If you still cannot log in, contact support with your account email and role.

### Account Security

Recommended security practices:

- Use a unique password.
- Do not share dashboard credentials.
- Update your password if you suspect unauthorized access.
- Keep your account email active.
- Log out on shared devices.

### Notifications

Notifications may include:

- Campaign status updates.
- Creator invitations.
- Application decisions.
- Submission review updates.
- Invoice and payment events.
- Support case replies.

Check the Notifications area regularly so you do not miss deadlines, feedback, or payout updates.

### Updating Email or Contact Details

If your email, phone number, billing contact, or primary contact person changes:

1. Update the relevant account or profile setting.
2. Save changes.
3. Confirm that dashboard notifications and email messages still reach the right person.
4. Contact support if you cannot change the email from your dashboard.

## Support Cases

Use support cases when an issue needs Huerray review or cannot be solved from the dashboard.

Good case titles:

- "Payout delayed for approved gig"
- "Cannot upload final video"
- "Invoice missing for campaign"
- "Need to add Creators to active campaign"
- "Revision request unclear"

Include:

- Your role: Creator or Brand.
- Related campaign, gig, invoice, or payment ID if available.
- A clear description of the issue.
- Screenshots or filenames when relevant.
- The action you need from Huerray.
- Any deadline or urgency.

Case priority guidance:

- Low: General question or non-urgent update.
- Medium: Workflow issue that does not block an immediate deadline.
- High: Payment, upload, access, or campaign issue affecting active work.
- Urgent: Time-sensitive production, payment, legal, or access issue that blocks delivery.

## FAQ

### Creator FAQ

#### How do I join a campaign?

Browse available gigs in the Creator dashboard, open a gig, review the requirements, and apply. You can also accept direct invitations from brands.

#### Do I need to post the content on my own channels?

No, not unless the campaign explicitly requires posting. Most Huerray UGC work is created for the brand to use.

#### Where do I submit content?

Open the active gig from your Creator dashboard and upload content through the submission area.

#### When will I get paid?

Payouts are processed after content approval. Existing platform FAQ content states that payments are transferred within 14 days once the Creator balance reaches EUR 50.

#### Can a brand request revisions?

Yes. A revision may be requested when the content needs changes within the original brief. If the brand requests additional deliverables or a major scope change, contact Huerray.

#### How do I update my profile?

Go to Creator dashboard > Profile and update your bio, categories, social links, portfolio information, and profile image.

#### How do I update bank details?

Go to Creator dashboard > Settings > Bank Settings or Bank Details, update the required information, and save.

#### What should I do if I cannot upload a video?

Check the file format, file size, internet connection, and required video specs. Try again, then submit a support case if the upload still fails.

### Brand FAQ

#### How do I create a campaign?

Go to Brand dashboard > Campaigns and create a new campaign. Add the brief, audience, content requirements, deliverables, timeline, and budget.

#### How do I invite Creators?

Open the Creators area or the relevant campaign, choose Creators that fit the brief, and send invitations.

#### How do I review submissions?

Open the campaign or gig, go to the submissions area, review the uploaded content, then approve it or request a revision.

#### Can I run multiple campaigns?

Yes. Manage active and past campaigns from the Campaigns section.

#### How does billing work?

Billing is handled per campaign or selected package. Invoices and payment history are available in the Brand dashboard under Invoices and Payments.

#### Can I add more Creators to an active campaign?

Yes, but if the added Creators exceed the original scope, contact Huerray so the campaign order and invoice can be updated.

#### How do I download approved content?

Open the relevant campaign or gig and download the final files from the approved submission or assets area once approval and payment requirements are complete.

#### Can I add teammates to my Brand account?

Yes. Contact Huerray with the names and email addresses of the colleagues who need access.
